# Expanded English dictionary provenance

`en.txt` contains 100,000 lowercase English words selected from SCOWL 2020.12.07 English and American word lists. SCOWL's license is in `tools/spellcheck/Copyright`. Candidates were ranked with wordfreq 3.1.1 and supplemented from SCOWL when needed. See `WORDFREQ_README.md` for wordfreq data licenses and source credits, including OpenSubtitles and the SUBTLEX authors. The wordfreq data is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.

The 171 non-ASCII entries originally selected were replaced by ASCII SCOWL entries because Leaf's compact English DAWG uses English letters and apostrophes.
